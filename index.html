History Guesser

About

Welcome to History Guesser. This game is inspired by posts I've seen on https://www.reddit.com/r/datemymap/ as well as games such as https://timeguessr.com/.

In this game you'll be presented with a world map at a specific date. The date ranges from to today by default, or you can turn the challenge up by enabling hard mode to have dates back to .

Because historic boundaries change multiple times in a year, the month and day will match today's month and day so that you only have to guess the year.

In daily mode you'll have rounds and get a score at the end of it based on how close you were to the correct years. You'll get a new puzzle each day.

You can also play practice mode for endless rounds.
