History Guesser

Welcome to History Guesser. This game is inspired by posts I've seen on https://www.reddit.com/r/datemymap/ as well as games such as https://timeguessr.com/.

In this game you'll be presented with a world map at a specific date. The date ranges from 1900 to today by default, or you can turn the challenge up by enabling hard mode to have dates back to 3300 BC

Because historic boundaries change multiple times in a year, the month and day will match today's month and day so that you only have to guess the year.

In daily mode you'll have 5 rounds and get a score at the end of it based on how close you were to the correct years. You'll get a new puzzle each day.

You can also play practice mode for endless rounds.

Map powered by https://www.runningreality.org/
